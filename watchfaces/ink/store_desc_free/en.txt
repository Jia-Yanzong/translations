Ink, A highly readable sports watch face featuring extra-large, bold digits for instant time visibility.

⭐️ Free vs PRO ⭐️
This watchface is available at no cost with all basic functions. Premium features (marked with [*]) require a Pro Key to unlock. You can purchase a Pro Key here: https://ko-fi.com/s/6f7f60cbba or https://apps.garmin.com/apps/e8a9d48e-dfa7-4b07-b69a-0a264adf6edd.

⭐️ Datafields ⭐️
All datafields that Garmin currently supplies to third party developers are available, and support for other third party apps.
All available datafields are listed here: https://watchface.io/docs/datafields. Some datafields (like training readiness or HRV) are currently not available to third party developers, see here: https://watchface.io/faq/can-you-add-training-readiness

⭐️ OWM / YR Weather on Instinct 3 Solar and Instinct E ⭐️
Install OWM Weather GB or YR Weather GB on your watch and this watch face shows its weather: temperature, forecast, wind, rain, UV index and more. Pick the app under "Weather app for the OWM/YR fields" in the settings.
* OWM Weather GB (OpenWeatherMap, needs your own API key): https://apps.garmin.com/apps/706f14e6-40f4-4aa2-805c-b88bd00bd9a9
* YR Weather GB (MET Norway, no key needed): https://apps.garmin.com/apps/1361482e-6f81-4ed5-97ee-e93d76f9849f
* More info: https://watchface.io/docs/owm-plugin and https://watchface.io/docs/yr-plugin
